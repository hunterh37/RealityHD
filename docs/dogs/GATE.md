# Dog realism gate

Renders: `animalpreview <species> --out out/x` (body sheet) and `--focus head|leg` (close-ups). Images in this folder are the final iteration.

Scored by eye against a written breed checklist (head, ears, topline, legs, tail, coat). Scale 0 to 10.

| Iteration | Golden | Shepherd | Corgi | Top defects fixed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (rigid lofts, `out/d2`) | 2 | 2 | 2 | plates at shoulder and hip, boxed legs, burlap coat |
| 1 (SDF coat) | 3 | 3 | 2 | manatee head, buried nose and eyes, 19 cm neck radius, rump ball |
| 2 | 4 | 4 | 3 | silky hanging ears, plume tail, feathering, striped legs |
| 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | erect furred ears, nose pad, corgi flank line and white legs, flipped-quad hole |

Open: coat reads as strand rows at close range, fur chart seams on the flank, legs thin for the golden.
